## About

[FreshRSS][freshrss] is a self-hosted RSS and Atom feed aggregator. It
collects the feeds you subscribe to, keeps track of what you have read, and
lets you search, label and filter your way through them.

It reads your feeds on a schedule, so what you open is already waiting for
you, and it speaks the Google Reader and Fever protocols, which lets mobile
readers like Reeder, FeedMe, Fluent Reader and NetNewsWire sync against it.

Home Assistant signs you in when you open it from the sidebar, so there is no
second login to get past.
